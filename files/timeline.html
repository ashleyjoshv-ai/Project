Timeline

  1. 1960s – Paul Baran (USA)
    Has the idea of a network that keeps working even if parts are broken.
  2. 1965 – Donald Davies (UK)
    Invents the word "packet".
  3. 1969 – ARPANET
    The first message is sent between two computers. It crashes after "LO", the first two letters of "LOGIN"!
  4. 1974 – Vint Cerf and Bob Kahn
    Design TCP/IP, the rules that let different networks talk to each other.
  5. 1983 – TCP/IP goes live
    ARPANET switches to TCP/IP on January 1st. This is often called the birth of the modern internet.
  6. Today
    Billions of packets travel the world every second. 🌍
